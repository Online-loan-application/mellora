Style plan: Soft-Trust Modernism
Keywords: calm, rounded, assured
Style description: A warm digital-banking identity that balances approachability with financial trust. Generous negative space, a compact monogram, and restrained typography keep the brand modern without feeling cold.
Font plan: Sora for the lowercase wordmark and headlines; DM Sans for labels and supporting copy.
Color scheme: Butter #FFEFB3 and deep green #013E37, used as exact inverse pairs with no extra accent colors.
Layout plan: Three square boards—primary horizontal lockup, reversed vertical lockup, and app-icon presentation—with centered hero elements and discreet editorial labels.
Core visual element requirements: A custom M monogram with sturdy uprights and a soft central V, enclosed in a generously rounded square. It must remain legible at app-icon scale and work in both color directions.
